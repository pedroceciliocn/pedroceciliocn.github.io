## About Me

I am an undergraduate student in Information Systems at the Federal University of Pernambuco (UFPE), with a strong interest in automation, web scraping, data analysis, software engineering, and AI (specifically in computer vision). I interned on the CIn/Motorola Project, where I worked directly with multidisciplinary teams from Brazil, the United States, India, and China, developing collaboration skills in a global environment to ensure software quality on mobile devices.
My experience included creating and maintaining test automation scripts, executing automated tests, and using tools such as Python, Linux, ADB, Jenkins, Docker, and Git. I also gained knowledge of agile methodologies. Additionally, I have experience in web development using Django and Flask, and in data analysis, including working with DataFrames and applying libraries such as Pandas, NumPy, and Matplotlib for data visualization and interpretation.
